Three steps, no copying

  1. Pick a calendar

    Your own or any shared calendar you can edit.

  2. Match your columns

    Title, date, time, duration. Rowdate guesses them from your headers and shows a preview.

  3. Sync

    Each row gets its event ID and status. Next time only changed rows are sent.

Formula dates, event updates and cancellations.

Keep a schedule in Google Sheets and send its rows to Calendar, with a status for each event.

  • Dates from formulas work =A2+7, =WORKDAY(), imported data: if Sheets shows a date, Rowdate reads it.
  • Edits update, not duplicate Each row remembers its event. Change the row, the same event changes.
  • Cancel deletes Put "yes" in a cancel column and the event is removed from the calendar.
  • Clear errors, per row "Date not recognised: 32.13.2026" in the row itself, not a silent failure.
  • Any date style 2026-11-10, 10.11.2026, 11/10/2026 by your sheet's locale, with or without time.
  • Narrow permissions Only the open spreadsheet and calendar events. No Gmail, no Drive, no servers of ours.

Questions

Does it work with formulas and imported data?

Yes. Rowdate reads the value Sheets calculated, in your spreadsheet's time zone. Text dates like 10.11.2026 or 2026-11-10 14:30 work too.

What happens if I delete an event in Calendar?

The next sync creates it again from the row. The sheet is the source of truth; to remove an event, use the cancel column.

Can it send invitations?

Yes, if you map a guest email column and tick "Email invitations to guests". Otherwise guests are added silently.

Where is my data stored?

In your spreadsheet and your calendar. Rowdate runs inside Google Apps Script and only talks to the Google Calendar API and our license checker.

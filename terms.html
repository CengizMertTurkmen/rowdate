The service

Rowdate creates, updates and deletes Google Calendar events from rows of your spreadsheet, using your own Google account. The free plan includes 100 new events per calendar month. Pro removes that limit and adds live sync.

Your responsibilities

  • Sync only data you are allowed to process, and invite only people who expect to hear from you.
  • Keep the Rowdate Event ID column intact; editing it can unlink rows from their events.
  • Check the preview before large syncs. Rowdate does what the sheet says, including deleting events marked cancelled.
